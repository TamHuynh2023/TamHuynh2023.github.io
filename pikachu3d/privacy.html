Tiny Beasts Onet 3D: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026 (the game was renamed from "Pikachu 3D"; nothing else changed)

This Privacy Policy explains how the game Tiny Beasts Onet 3D (package com.tamhuynh.pikachu3d, "the game") handles information. The game is made by an independent developer, Tam Huynh ("we").

Summary

  • The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat and no in-app purchases.
  • We do not collect, store or sell any personal information.
  • Your game progress and settings are saved only on your device.
  • The game shows optional rewarded video ads (Google AdMob) only when the player taps Hint or Revive. All ad requests are marked as directed to children.

Information saved on your device

The game saves your progress (cleared levels, stars), your settings (music, sound effects, graphics quality) and a technical log file on your device. This data never leaves your device, and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to show rewarded video ads. An ad plays only when the player chooses one. Every ad request is tagged "child-directed" and "under the age of consent". As a result:

  • ads are not personalised and no interest-based advertising or remarketing is used;
  • only ad sources certified for Google Play's Families policy are used;
  • ad content is limited to the G (general audiences) rating;
  • the game does not use the Android Advertising ID (the AD_ID permission is removed).

To deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may automatically process limited technical information, such as the IP address (used for approximate location), device type, operating system version and how the ad was interacted with. Google handles this information under its own policies:

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • AdMob and children: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6223431

The game works offline. Without an internet connection no ads are loaded, and Hint is free.

Children

The game is suitable for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. We comply with Google Play's Families policy and, through the settings described above, with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the GDPR rules on children's data. If you believe a child's personal information has been collected through the game, contact us and we will help to have it removed.

Permissions

The game requests only network permissions (and the standard background-work permissions that Google's ad library adds), which are needed to load ads. It does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files or precise location.

Your rights

Because we do not hold personal information, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. For data processed by Google for advertising, see the Google links above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
